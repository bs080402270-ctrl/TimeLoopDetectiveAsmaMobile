# Privacy Policy — Time Loop Detective

Last updated: 2026-09-19

Time Loop Detective is designed to work offline.

The current version does not require an account and does not intentionally collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device.

The app does not currently include advertising, analytics, location tracking, contacts access, microphone access, camera access, or third-party sign-in.

Future versions that add online services will update this policy before those services are enabled.
