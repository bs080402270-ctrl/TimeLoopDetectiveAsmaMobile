# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name
Time Loop Detective

## Short description
Solve a murder across three repeating time loops. Evidence remembers what everyone else forgets.

## Full description
The clock always reaches 8:52.

Daniel Rowan dies inside The Daily Bean, the rain reverses, the cups refill, and the night begins again. Everyone forgets what happened—except you.

Investigate a cinematic mystery across a repeating timeline. Question suspects, search atmospheric locations, collect evidence, expose contradictions and rebuild the final minutes before the crime.

Features:
- A complete detective case designed around evidence, not random guessing
- Four suspects with motives, secrets and changing testimony
- Eight pieces of evidence
- Three time loops where knowledge survives
- Timeline and casebook tools
- Multiple endings based on the strength of your deduction
- Offline play with local autosave
- Mobile-first illustrated presentation

Can you prove what happened before the clock resets again?
