# Time Loop Detective — Case 01 Game Design

## Core fantasy
The player is a detective trapped inside the final 37 minutes before Daniel Rowan dies at The Daily Bean. The world resets at 8:52 PM, but evidence and player knowledge survive.

## Case
**The Last Cup**

Daniel Rowan is preparing to expose financial fraud. At 8:31 PM his usual drink is altered. At 8:43 PM the office breaker is bridged. At 8:47 PM the lights fail. At 8:49 PM Daniel collapses.

The solution is evidence-driven. Lina Vale altered Daniel's order with an ingredient she knew could trigger his severe allergy, then engineered the blackout to retrieve incriminating financial records during the confusion.

## Suspects
- Maya Cole — cafe manager. Daniel plans to sell the cafe.
- Omar Hale — journalist. Secretly recording Daniel and initially conceals evidence.
- Lina Vale — business partner. True culprit; financial motive and access.
- Theo Marsh — barista. Has suspicious key access, but timing clears him.

## Evidence
1. Altered Receipt — establishes means/opportunity around the drink.
2. Broken Watch — fixes blackout at 8:47.
3. Wet Umbrella — rear-alley movement.
4. Violet Thread — places Lina at rear corridor.
5. Recorded Argument — supplies audit motive and helps clear Omar.
6. Breaker Dust — shows blackout was deliberate.
7. Missing Ledger Page — establishes financial motive.
8. Spare Office Key — creates and then resolves Theo suspicion.

## Three-loop structure
### Loop 1 — Observe
Learn the cast, locations, and public stories.

### Loop 2 — Challenge
Use retained evidence to press suspects. Contradictions unlock.

### Loop 3 — Prove
Complete the evidence chain and make the final deduction.

## Systems
- Location travel
- Suspect interrogation
- Evidence discovery
- Contradiction checks
- Persistent casebook
- Timeline review
- Loop reset
- Autosave
- Three ending qualities
- Mobile-first portrait UI

## Endings
- True ending: accuse Lina with strong evidence and expose her contradiction.
- Partial ending: identify Lina without a complete proof chain.
- Wrong accusation: accuse another suspect or accuse without adequate support.

## Expansion hooks
The engine and data layout are case-oriented so future mysteries can add new JSON case files, locations, suspects and evidence without rebuilding the core interface.
