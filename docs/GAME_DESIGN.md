# Time Loop Detective — Season 1 Design

## Core fantasy
The player is Asma's main detective partner and is physically present in the mystery.

## Delivery rules
- Keep narration short.
- Use natural dialogue and brief scene descriptions.
- Reveal information through conversation, investigation, clues and choices.
- Suspects have hidden motives, contradictions and tells.
- Asma may disagree with the player when evidence points elsewhere.

## Time loop
The world resets but player knowledge persists. Later loops unlock observations and evidence that could not be understood earlier. Asma remembers the player's investigative direction. The deeper cause of the loop is a season-wide mystery and should not be explained early.

## Visual events
Supported visual kinds: crime_scene, location, interrogation, clue, search, chase, pursuit, cover, confrontation, capture and loop.

Important actions should change the visual scene. Dedicated artwork overrides safe fallback artwork.

## Outcomes
Every case can reach true, partial or wrong outcomes. Final confrontations can branch through chase, restraint/fight, handcuffs or unlocked equipment.
