# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name
Time Loop Detective: Asma

## Short description
Solve ten mysteries with Asma. Remember what the time loop erases.

## Full description
You are not watching Detective Asma solve the case. You are sitting beside her.

Time Loop Detective: Asma is a conversation-driven mystery game where you investigate crimes, question suspects, search locations, collect evidence, chase leads, expose contradictions and survive repeating timelines.

Every loop changes what you can learn.

A clue that looks meaningless in the first cycle may become critical in the second. A suspect can repeat the same story with one detail changed. Asma remembers the direction of your investigation and may agree with you, challenge you, or tell you when the evidence does not support your theory.

Season 1 includes ten connected mysteries. Each case contributes fragments to a larger mystery about why the timeline keeps repeating.

### Features
- Ten Season 1 detective cases
- Detective Asma as your active investigation partner
- Natural, short dialogue instead of long narration
- Multiple suspects, hidden motives and contradictions
- Loop-gated clues and discoveries
- Persistent evidence and player decisions across resets
- Suspect observation and body-language clues
- Search, pursuit, chase, cover and confrontation scenes
- Evidence casebook and timeline reconstruction
- Multiple case outcomes
- Season-wide time-loop mystery
- Offline play and local autosave
- Mobile-first portrait presentation

The world resets.

Your evidence does not.

What will you notice next time?
